About me

I'm a high-school student and a hobbyist specializing in technology and software development.

I got my start by making videogames, two of which I have self-published. I continued on to write many projects throughout the years, including a series of chemistry apps as a school project. Now, my interests include artificial intelligence, theoretical computer science, low-level programming, and philosophy.

Above all, I seek depth, mastery, and perseverance*. I believe the greatest financial AND spiritual contributions are generated by individuals that steadfastly and rigorously hold to these three values, and so I am working to cultivate these three in myself so that I, too, can truly help make the world a few percentage points better.

  • DEPTH - how far you explore.
  • MASTERY - how well you know what you've explored.
  • PERSEVERANCE - how much you can keep going.
